# Stabilize After Broad Prerelease Validation

The library will remain an explicitly non-adoptable `0.x` prerelease while its broad conventional
catalog, Markdown editor, chart family, and four integration slices challenge the public
foundation. Stable `1.0` and the default package tag require explicit acceptance after the full
behavioral, accessibility, visual, type, package, performance, browser, SSR, hydration, and bundle
isolation gates pass; normal semantic compatibility begins only then. Individual prerelease
tickets automate browser accessibility-tree and interaction checks, while manual screen-reader
exercises are consolidated in the release-wide hardening gate immediately before `1.0`.

Ticket-level evidence and review are risk-based and bounded by documented supported behavior. One
complete review is followed by targeted review of any corrections and the behavior they can affect,
not repeated unrestricted searches for new edge cases. Unsupported permutations and speculative
hardening become later planning inputs rather than delivery blockers; supported-path security,
data-loss, and accessibility failures remain blocking. Repeated waves of new findings require
clarifying the ticket boundary before more remediation.

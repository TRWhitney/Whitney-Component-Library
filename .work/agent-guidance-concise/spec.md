# Measure shorter guidance against native authoring

Historical study plan. Raw captures and obsolete replay setups were later removed under the
[retention policy](../../docs/research/agent-evidence-retention.md). Capture instructions below describe execution, not a retention requirement.

## Problem Statement

Whitney's aim is clearer phrasing that needs fewer words and reduces agent token cost. The previous
expanded example experiment reduced observed runtime reading without consistent completed savings.

## Solution

Compare current guidance, a shorter wording-only candidate and plain React/CSS on three existing
realistic tasks, with two repetitions per condition. Present all 18 outcomes for review.

## User Stories

1. As Whitney, I want guidance-size savings distinguished from completed authoring cost.
2. As a consuming agent, I want concise guidance with the same supported behavior and exact props.

## Implementation Decisions

Eight task-relevant usage pages may change only in disposable packages. Preserve full contracts,
exact props, examples, links, package discovery, runtime/CSS and other pages. Reuse existing profile
editor, contact form and people-directory briefs. Agents choose their own reading routes.

## Testing Decisions

Freeze the [protocol](../../docs/research/agent-guidance-concise-protocol.md), candidate and sources
before launching. Preflight actual archives and compare installed trees. Measure prose words,
complete-page reference tokens, completed provider counters including repairs, uptake and reading
routes. Independently evaluate at 390px and 1280px, then review the evidence and commit.

## Out of Scope

Production adoption, structural retrieval changes, metadata/runtime/API edits, README/AGENTS or
showcase edits, pushing/publishing, additional author attempts, and claims of general savings.

## Further Notes

Whitney authorized the proposed 18-attempt comparison with "please continue with that" and
clarified that clearer wording should reduce words. Standing authorization permits liberal commits
of completed experimental outcomes. Production guidance adoption remains for review.

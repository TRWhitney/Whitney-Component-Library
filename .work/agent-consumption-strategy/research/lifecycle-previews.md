# Rendering findings

The [results](lifecycle-results.md) preserve the recorded layout findings and correctness limits.
Inspection covered matched initial/final states at 390px and 1280px, both appearances, visible
keyboard focus, long-name wrapping and edit dialogs. No additional clipping, wrapping or focus
defect required a correction round. These observations are not subjective style scores.

Images, structured captures, source snapshots and the historical replay setup were removed under
the [retention policy](../../../docs/research/agent-evidence-retention.md). The consumer apps cannot
be reconstructed from this tree. The retained briefs and checker can support a fresh comparison.
